_Last updated: June 2025_

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Automatically collected data

- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- IP address and approximate region
- Pages visited and timestamps
- Referring URLs

### 1.2 Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies help us remember your theme preferences, keep sessions consistent, and analyze overall site usage.

## 2. How we use your information

We process the collected data to:

- Provide and maintain the site
- Improve performance and content relevance
- Analyze usage trends and diagnose issues
- Respond when you contact us

## 3. Sharing your data

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Information is only shared when:

- Required by law or legal process
- Necessary for operating the site with trusted infrastructure providers
- You explicitly request or consent to it

## 4. Data retention

Analytics data is stored only for as long as needed to operate the blog and monitor performance. Email conversations are retained for reference unless you request deletion.

## 5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Please email [gaazeon@gaazeon.com] with your request.

## 6. Third-party services

We use privacy-friendly analytics (currently Umami) that does not store personally identifiable information. If new services are added, they will follow the same standard.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Major changes will be announced via the blog, and the “Last updated” date above will be revised accordingly.

If you have any questions about privacy, please contact us at [gaazeon@gaazeon.com].
