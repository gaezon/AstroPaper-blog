Thank you for visiting Gaazeon's blog! If you have questions, suggestions, or would like to collaborate, feel free to reach out.

## 📧 Email

**Primary inbox**: [gaazeon@gaazeon.com]

I usually reply within 24–48 hours. Adding a short summary in the subject line helps me respond faster.

## 📝 Common reasons to reach out

### Technical questions

- Issues related to posts or sample code
- Questions about implementation details
- Tooling recommendations
- Open-source collaboration ideas

### Content feedback

- Corrections or clarifications
- Suggestions for improvements
- Topic requests
- Translation assistance

### Partnerships

- Guest articles or sponsored posts
- Product review requests
- Other collaboration proposals

## ⏰ Response time

- **Email**: typically 1–2 business days
- **Social media**: usually the same day
- **Urgent technical issues**: as soon as possible

## 📋 Tips for faster assistance

When you get in touch, please:

1. **Describe the issue clearly** — include error messages, environment details, or screenshots.
2. **Share relevant code snippets** if it is a programming question.
3. **Mention the desired outcome** so I understand what you're aiming to achieve.
4. **Allow some time** — I’ll get back to you as quickly as I can.

## 🔒 Privacy

- Your personal information stays private.
- Email addresses are never shared or used for marketing.
- With your permission, technical discussions might be anonymized and turned into future posts.

## 📢 FAQ

**Q: May I republish your articles?**  
Yes, please provide attribution and a link to the original post. For commercial use, contact me first.

**Q: Do you offer paid consulting?**  
I currently focus on community discussions. For more complex engagements, send me the details by email.

**Q: How can I follow new posts?**  
Subscribe via RSS or follow the linked social accounts for updates.
